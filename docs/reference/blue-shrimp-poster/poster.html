Blue Jelly & Blue Dream

Neocaridina davidi · two blue colour morphs, one planted tank · anatomy and animation study

Built for grazing and swimming. Walking legs and abdominal swimming limbs are separate groups. The first two walking-leg pairs help handle food. [1] The current game rig groups these features into five mesh parts.

Behaviour to suggest. These shrimp spend much of their time grazing on biofilms. [1] Resting, crawling and brief swimming excursions are the game’s authored motion states; tail-flick timing is a tunable.

BIOLOGY · SOURCED

Small freshwater grazers

This species originates from Taiwan; selective breeding produced the ornamental colour morphs. Adults can reach 3–4 cm. [1]

The clear silhouette should retain dark eyes, antennae and a segmented abdomen. Internal markings in the Jelly drawing are restrained visual cues, not a complete anatomical reconstruction.
