# Thai reef: colour references for the creature skins

8 November 2026 · Modelling guidance for W0/W2 of the [reef finishing plan](../plans/2026-11-08-reef-tanks-finish.md). Nothing here is printed on the poster. Colours vary between individuals and colour forms, so each skin follows the description that most sources share and records where they disagree. The species occurrence records live in the separate reef research ledger; this file covers only appearance.

| Animal (tank) | What the skin shows | Sources | Disagreements |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Spirobranchus* sp., Christmas tree worms | Eight colour morphs: blue, red, orange, yellow, white, purple and two-tone; radioles banded root to tip (done in W1) | — (polychromatism of the genus is the modelling basis; W1 approved by Will, 2026‑11‑08) | — |
| *Trapezia cymodoce*, coral guard crab | Smooth, polished carapace, purple-brown to orange, with a row of small orange-red spots; dark claw fingers and joints | [Western Australian Museum](https://museum.wa.gov.au/online-collections/node/107741) ("dorsal surface purple to brown, with a row of orange/red spots"); [JungleDragon](https://www.jungledragon.com/specie/16857/red-coral-crab.html) (orange with yellow; dots on a darker carapace); [Meerwasser-Lexikon](https://en.reeflex.net/tiere/2156_Trapezia_cymodoce.htm) ("dark claws and joints") | The lexicon calls it light brown; the [Naturalis key](https://crabs-japan.linnaeus.naturalis.nl/linnaeus_ng/app/views/species/taxon.php?id=34170) says the carapace lacks *regular* spots. The skin uses a brown-orange base with sparse, irregular orange-red spots |
| *Stenopus hispidus*, banded coral shrimp ×2 | Alternating red and white bands across the body, claws and legs; long white antennae | Species already approved for its "form and coloration" (Will, 2026‑11‑02, original plan); the bands are in the mesh geometry | — |
| *Phyllidia varicosa*, nudibranch | Black body with 3–6 raised blue-grey longitudinal ridges, the tubercles on them capped in yellow; yellow rhinophores | [Plazi treatment](https://tb.plazi.org/GgServer/html/03B5F62B4735FFA7E3BEFE99A7B29B29) (black, light blue-grey ridges topped with deep yellow tubercles); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Phyllidia_varicosa) (blue-grey ridge and tubercle bases, yellow caps); [Meerwasser-Lexikon](https://eng.reeflex.net/tiere/2300_Phyllidia_varicosa.htm) (3–5 blue-grey to pale grey ridges) | Older accounts give orange rather than yellow tubercles. The skin uses yellow with an orange tip |
| *Dardanus lagopodes*, hermit crab | Mottled tan, red-brown and maroon claws and legs with white-tipped dark bristles and a dark "knee" patch; it lives in a borrowed gastropod shell | [Reef Life Survey](https://reeflifesurvey.com/species/dardanus-lagopodes/) (mottled tan and maroon, dark hairs tipped white); [Solitary Islands URG](https://www.surg.org.au/species/dardanus-lagopodes-forsskal-1775/) (black and red forms); [Marine Life Photography](https://marinelifephotography.com/marine/arthropods/hermit%20crabs/dardanus-lagopodes.htm) (white legs with reddish-brown splashes, black spots above the second segment) | Leg colour differs between forms; the skin uses the red-brown form. The shell is a generic gastropod shell (brown and cream spiral growth lines), not a species claim |
| *Sabellastarte* sp., feather dusters ×4 | Radioles banded dark brown and buff; tough, leathery tube coated in fine mud | [Bishop Museum guide](https://data.bishopmuseum.org/HBS/invertguide/species/sabellastarte_spectabilis.htm) (dark brown and buff bands; leathery, mud-covered tube); [eNature Qatar](https://www.enature.qa/specie/sabellastarte-spectabilis/) (dark and pale brown stripes) | The congener *S. magnifica* varies from brown to purple or gold; the skin uses the brown-and-buff banding both sources share |
