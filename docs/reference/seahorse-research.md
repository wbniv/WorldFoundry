# Longsnout seahorse research — 16 October 2026

Research for the saltwater seahorses ([plan](../plans/2026-10-16-saltwater-seahorses.md), Phase 0). It was written before the A3 poster, which prints only claims from this document. The subject is the longsnout seahorse, *Hippocampus reidi* Ginsburg 1933. Other species appear only where no *H. reidi* data were found, and they are named each time.

## Status vocabulary

The four statuses follow the [2026‑10‑06 poster audit](2026-10-06-aquarium-poster-reference-verification.md):

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **verified** | I read the source and it says this about *H. reidi*. The "Read" column says whether that was the full text or only the abstract. |
| **other species** | I read the source, but its subject is another species (or the whole family). The species is named. |
| **unverified** | No primary source was read: the source was unreachable or paywalled, or only popular accounts exist. These claims stay here and do not appear on the poster. |
| **ours** | A game choice: an authored number, behaviour or estimate. It is not a measurement of a seahorse. |

All sources were opened on 2026‑10‑16. Page numbers are the printed page numbers.

**Our numbers against these.** The plan's per-joint limits are linear from ring 0 to ring 34: ventral 12°→40°, dorsal 4°→12.5° (raised from 10° after this review), lateral 8°→28°, twist 3°→8°. Summed over 35 joints they allow 910° ventral, about 289° dorsal (245° before the change), 630° lateral and 192.5° of twist.

- Ventral (910°) and lateral (630°) are within about 7 % and 11 % of *H. kuda*'s whole-tail values (P6). Ventral is within 14 % of P7's 800°. Ventral as the dominant direction matches P6 and the "predominantly bend ventrally" statement in P5.
- **Dorsal is low.** 245° is 16 % below *H. kuda*'s 290° (P6) and far below P7's 600°. Raising the tip limit from 10° to about 13° would reach 290°. **Adopted 2026‑10‑16:** the plan raised the tip limit to 12.5°, which gives 288.75°.
- **Twist is plausible.** Our per-joint twist (3°→8°) stays under the square prototype's ~15° per segment (P4). That is a model's limit, not a live tail's.
- **More motion toward the tip** is supported by R5 below and by P5's logarithmic spiral. No source gives a per-segment range of motion for *H. reidi*, so every per-joint limit stays **ours**.

## Findings that change the plan's claims table

1. **Dorsal fin frequency.** The plan's 30–42 Hz is *H. erectus* (as *H. hudsonius*, 2 animals): **other species** (F3). For *H. reidi*, the fins beat at 20–60 Hz depending on activity, most often about 50 Hz (F1, F2; conference proceedings).
2. **Strike time.** "Under 5 ms" is a general background statement (K2). Adult *H. reidi* takes 5.7 ± 0.8 ms from hyoid onset until the prey crosses the mouth (K1).
3. **Ring count.** 35 is the modal *H. reidi* tail-ring count (R1), not only the middle of the range. The range is 31–39 (R1) or 30–39 (R2); 33–37 (R3) is one study's narrower range. The 11 trunk rings are verified.
4. **Colour source.** The reef-guide page has no colour text (S9). Colour is now sourced from Lourie p. 72 and Silveira 2014 (S7, S8). "Yellow to orange, brown, black" remains unverified.
5. **Greeting.** Unverified; the primary source is *H. whitei* and is paywalled (G3–G5). Pairs in the wild are verified (G1).
6. **Eyes.** Family-level statement, chipped other species (E1).
7. **Joint limits.** Ventral and lateral sums agree with *H. kuda* within about 11 % (P6). The dorsal sum (245°) is lower than *H. kuda*'s 290° (P6) and P7's 600°. The plan now uses a 12.5° dorsal tip limit (288.75° in total).
8. **Stocking.** The Ocean Rider figure is a 2006 forum reply about *H. erectus*. The "unless experienced" caveat in the plan's Context was not found (T1–T3).
9. **Tail proportion.** No adult *H. reidi* source was found. 90 mm is 56 % of a 16 cm height; it stays ours (S6).
