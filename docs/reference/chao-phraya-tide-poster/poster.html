The tide at Bangkok

How a computer adds up the river’s tide, and how far to trust the sum
The Chao Phraya at Bangkok · World Foundry harbour clock

verified Station HD, Royal Thai Navy Headquarters, 13° 44′ 36″ N 100° 29′ 25″ E, on the river inside Bangkok. Heights: metres above the lowest low water. [1]

verified The Navy posts every day’s predicted highs and lows. It says its constants come from one year of gauge readings. [1, 2]

ours (measured) 2026 lists 1225 highs and lows between 0.53 m and 3.33 m. A typical day swings 1.65 m; the largest swing is 2.59 m.

01 · Two highs a day, or only one

ours (measured) Of 365 days in 2026, 238 list two highs and two lows, 92 list one of each, and 35 list one or three events.

ours (measured) Where a day lists one high there is a flat shoulder where a second would be. The table lists only true tops and bottoms, so events can be 17 hours apart.

other source A form factor (K1 + O1) ÷ (M2 + S2) from 0.25 to 1.5 makes a tide “mixed, mainly semidiurnal”. That paper’s model gives this station 1.06. [3]

ours (measured) Our fit gives 1.36: the same class.

02 · A tide is a sum of waves

verified A prediction adds cosine curves, h = Ho + Σ f·H·cos[a·t + (V0+u) − K], one for each cause. a is the cause’s speed. [4]

verified M2, the Moon’s main effect, makes two peaks every 24 hours 50 minutes. S2, the Sun’s, makes two every 24 hours. [4]

ours (measured) K1 50.0 cm (23.93 h), M2 36.3 cm (12.42 h), O1 30.9 cm (25.82 h), S2 23.1 cm (12.00 h): the four largest of 64. Periods come from NOAA’s speeds. [5]

03 · Spring and neap tides

ours (measured) M2 and S2 drift in and out of step every 14.77 days, the gap between their speeds. [5]

ours (measured) Added, they swing in amplitude from 59 cm (spring) to 13 cm (neap).

04 · A year of high and low water

ours (measured) The highest high and lowest low of each day of 2026, 0.53 m to 3.33 m. The middle of our fit is 2.29 m.

ours (measured) A yearly wave of 17 cm lifts and lowers the mean level. In a river it mixes the sea’s season with the river’s flow; two years cannot separate them.

verified At the river mouth, Bangkok Bar has monthly means from 1926. PSMSL flags them “not research quality data”. [6]

05 · From a table to constants

verified The Navy’s own constants are not published. It describes constants from one year of observations, and 112 constituents. [2, 7]

ours (measured) We fit its published highs and lows. At each event the height equals the listed height and the slope is zero, which is linear in the unknowns: one least squares finds all 64 waves from 171 candidates.

ours (measured) The 55 standard candidates use astronomical speeds that match NOAA’s table to 1e-7 degrees an hour; the rest are sums and differences a river makes. [5]

06 · How good is it?

ours (measured) Fitted to 2026, scored on 2027, which it never saw: median error 4.5 cm, 90th percentile 11.3 cm, largest 22.6 cm. The reverse: 4.1, 10.6, 21.5 cm.

ours (measured) Waves kept: 12 leave 21 cm, 24 leave 11 cm, 48 leave 5.6 cm, 64 leave 4.5 cm.

ours (measured) Times are looser: at a flat top a few centimetres move the peak by an hour or two. The median error in the time of a high or low is 13 min.

07 · Which clock does the table use?

ours (measured) Ko Lak’s observed 2025 hourly sea level, run forward, matches the Navy’s 2026 table to 11 min when the table is read 7 hours ahead of the record. With no shift the mismatch is 308 min.

verified The GLOSS handbook gives Ko Lak’s time zone as “105E, GMT + 7hrs”. [8]

ours (measured) The record’s own clock is not stated on the pages read. If it is GMT, as such archives usually are, the table is in Thai local time, UTC+7.

08 · In the harbour, and what we do not know

design (ours) In the Tide Harbour clock, one number moves the river, the boats on their ropes, the raft and a gauge board. The dial on the tower tells the time.

ours (measured) NOAA’s formula carries a yearly amplitude factor f; ours sets it to 1. One year on lost almost nothing: 4.5 cm on the fitted year, 4.5 cm on the next. [4]

limit (ours) River flow is absorbed into the yearly wave and the compound terms, not modelled. Nothing here is about the Sun’s or Moon’s position.
