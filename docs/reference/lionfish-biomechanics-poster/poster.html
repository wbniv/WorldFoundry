01 · Model the silhouette

Deep head, tapering trunk, rounded tail and distinct soft fins. Keep the eye on the skull; open the jaw and throat rather than splitting the entire face. Use 13 tapered dorsal spines and fleshy head tabs. [1, 2]

IMPLEMENTED: 4,068 triangles and one visual actor per fish, with named regions and independent UVs. 13 dorsal, 3 anal and paired pelvic spines.

02 · Paint a surface, not rings

Uneven red-brown/cream body bands; spotted soft dorsal, anal and tail fins. [1] Separate texture coordinates from deformation weights.

IMPLEMENTED: same 256² body/fin maps for every fish; individual stable palettes, including future spawns. Pattern/UVs/alpha stay identical; use existing translucency for fin membranes.

03 · Fins respond to the task

Field observations distinguish relaxed posture from travel with fins/spines folded back and more rapid tail strokes. [3] Smooth transitions; keep the roots stable and let curved tips lag.

04 · One coordinated gulp

Suction feeding combines gape, jaw rotation, head motion and hyoid depression. [4] Expansion drives a brief local flow: prey accelerates and can escape. Transfer ownership only on mouth entry; preserve inertia and off-axis convergence. No position tween.

GAME STARTING POINT: 0.26 s action / 0.10 s target are game choices. Actual capture requires aperture entry; misses remain possible. Review timing against footage.

2026 jet-blowing evidence concerns P. miles: useful context, not an automatic behavior rule for this P. volitans asset. [5]
