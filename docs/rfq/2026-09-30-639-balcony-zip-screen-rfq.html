Request for quotation — motorised zip screen for a condo balcony

Bangkok condominium, unit 639 (6th floor, ≈ 16 m above ground). Prepared 2026‑09‑30. Contact: Will Norris, wbnorris@gmail.com.

What I want

One outdoor zip screen (cassette + side zip guides), motorised with a solar‑strip motor (battery motor charged by a thin solar strip; no mains wiring on the balcony). It closes the opening between the pony wall and the concrete ledge above it. Please quote supply and installation.

Site conditions

  • Faces due west: strong afternoon sun and heat; monsoon rain; squall gusts on the 6th floor.
  • Goal: cut heat and glare and keep rain and wind out, while keeping some view.
  • Screen closes on the pony wall cap (bottom bar seals on it). The cassette goes under the concrete ledge, flush with the outer face of the pony wall.

Questions for every shop

  1. Which brand and model? Made in Thailand or imported (from where)? Lead time?
  2. Wind rating: which standard and what test size? Does it cover about 2.7 m wide?
  3. Solar strip: how many watts, on what battery (chemistry, Wh)? What temperature is the battery rated for? The cassette sits in direct afternoon sun. How many open/close cycles per day between charges? How long is the battery expected to last, and can it be replaced?
  4. Is there a mains or USB way to charge it in the rainy season?
  5. Warranty in years for the frame, fabric, motor, battery and solar strip. Who services it, and how fast?
  6. Do you need approval from the condo's juristic person? Can you supply drawings and a colour chip for that?
  7. What is included in the price: measure, delivery, installation, remote, sensor, VAT, payment terms?
