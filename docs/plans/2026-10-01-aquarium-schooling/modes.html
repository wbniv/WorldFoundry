Mockup 2: school and swarm, the rules and the switch

frozen snapshots of the mockup-1 simulation (seeded, so they never change); the numbers are the proposed tunables

SCHOOL: the leader is cruising

RuleWhat the follower does
slotseeks its own place behind and beside the leader (a loose V, two ranks), so the school never overlaps
alignmatches the leader's heading and speed (catches up with a burst if it falls behind)
separatepushes off any fish nearer than 0.9 body lengths (always on)

SWARM: the leader is resting or drifting

RuleWhat the follower does
orbitmills round the leader on its own shell (1.6 to 3.8 body lengths) with a slow swirl: the leader is a hub, not a head
wandera small per-fish drift (own phase), so the swarm breathes and never freezes
separatethe same push-off; no alignment, so the headings are scattered
The switch. Mode follows the leader's speed through a hysteresis: SCHOOL when it exceeds 0.9 m/s; back to SWARM only after it stays under 0.4 m/s for 0.5 s. The rules are blended by a value that eases toward 0 or 1 (about 0.6 s), so a school loosens into a swarm instead of snapping. Always on, both modes: separation, walls and floor and surface (soft push inside 0.9 m), the anemone crown (steer round, below), a turn-rate limit, and the same fixed-point-safe maths as the player. Measured in the tests: polarisation (how aligned the headings are) at least 0.8 in SCHOOL and at most 0.5 in SWARM; no pair closer than 0.5 body lengths; nobody through a wall.
