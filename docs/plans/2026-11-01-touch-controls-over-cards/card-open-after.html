Four realms await: Love, Reason, Truth and God. Begin with Love. Walk toward the red portal and press Select. Directions move and turn. A short Select press interacts; hold Select to switch between third person and first person. Keep holding Select to open level settings. The camera changes; your choices do not. Approach a speaker or object to interact. You can revisit the arguments before choosing a doorway. Each realm keeps its own doors, and each door asks a question of the one who walks through it. First Love waits on the street of Love with a story about the morning she met him, and Reason keeps a ledger of every argument you have heard so far. When a card runs to a second page, Select turns the page and the counter at the top right shows how far you have read. Truth and God are further on; their doors open only once Love and Reason have been heard, and the street remembers which arguments you chose to hear and which you walked past.
