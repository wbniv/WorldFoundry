Tide Harbour: tide data you can read   proposal, not built

The tide board, close up
Top row: the next high water. Bottom row: the next low water. Each has its time, to 10 minutes, and its height, to 0.1 m above the lowest low water. Arrow: the tide is rising or falling now.
All of it comes from the same 64 waves that move the river, stepped forward to the next turn. No table, no network.
13 rigid actors (proposed, untested): 12 digit drums that turn to a numeral, and the arrow. In the scene the board is about 315 × 158 pixels on a 1080p TV.
hour
A prediction, not a measurement. The constants are fitted to the Royal Thai Navy Hydrographic Department's published table for Bangkok: within 4 to 5 cm and 13 minutes of it (median; 43 minutes at the 90th percentile), which is why the board rounds to 10 minutes. Rain and river discharge move the real river and are not in it.
Found while drawing this: the model wiggles at flat tops: 1440 turns in 2026 where the table lists 1225. The board ignores turns under 4 cm, which leaves 1224 (count_turns.py). Grey marks on the curve are turns it skipped.
