A — Follow device (today)

Portrait-held phone: the 16:9 scene is a strip in the middle of a black screen. Stays available as follow-device.

B — Landscape (new default)

The Dream asks for landscape; the phone shows the scene full screen, even though it is held upright. landscape.

C — Sensor landscape

Like B, but follows the sensor between the two landscape directions, so the scene is never upside down. sensor-landscape.

How the request is madeAndroid 11 and later: DreamActivity.setRequestedOrientation. Android 10 and earlier: TYPE_DREAM window attribute (source-checked only).
Pixel-class phones, drawn to the same scale. The Chromecast TVs are always landscape and look the same in A, B and C.
