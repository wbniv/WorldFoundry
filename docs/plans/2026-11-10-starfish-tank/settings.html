Notes

Editable rows carry no marker. The read-only row is dimmed with a padlock.

Counts, Speed, Glass climbing and Caustics apply live: the Director polls the settings once a second.

Seed applies on the next entry. 0 draws new resident spots each time; any other value repeats them.

The same form opens from the Dream tank list and the TV level. Both read one catalog, SeaStarsSettings.
