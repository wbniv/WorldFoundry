Residents: the states each one shows

One director (Forth) runs every resident on timers seeded per level load; two react to the player.

Sand-sifting star

Buried. Under the sand: only a faint five-point outline and a bump.
Out. Rises in 4 s, glides 20–40 cm, sinks again. Touching it makes it sink at once.

Green brittle star

Hiding. Disc under the ledge; two or three arm tips out on the sand, waving.
Bolting. Player within 25 cm: it rows away on two arm pairs (fast, ×6 the stars) to another ledge.

Cushion star

Rest. A pentagonal cushion; no arms to bend.
Puff. Swells 8 % and settles over 10 s. Transforms only; no DFRM.

Chocolate-chip / Fromia

Graze. Chocolate-chip stars wander the sand between grazing spots.
Creep. Fromia creep along authored routes on the rock faces.
