Screensaver: aquarium_sea_stars_screensaver

The Dream variant swaps the player script for the autopilot (sea_stars_screensaver.fth) and carries the SeaStarsSettings object. Full screen, no HUD, no input.

Defaults: autopilot climbing the front glass

Maximum population: autopilot on the sand

One autopilot cycle (seeded waypoints)

0:00
enter: seeded spots
0:20
glide to sand spot
1:10
pause 10–40 s
2:30
up the front glass
4:00
along the back glass
5:30
rear up (reach)
…
repeat; no HUD, no input
The Dream picks Sea Stars from the phone list; the settings form opens from that list. Leaving the Dream restores the TV; the settings stay as set.
