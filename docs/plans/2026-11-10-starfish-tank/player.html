The player: a blue sea star, Linckia laevigata

Five arms are five DFRM v2 curves in one mesh. The star never swims: it holds to the sand or a glass plane.

On the sand

Any arm can lead. The arm nearest the stick direction lifts its tip (eyespot) and the star glides that way; the body turns at most 36° to line it up.

On the front glass

Push into the front glass and the star climbs it: you see its underside, the five grooves of tube feet and the mouth. ▲▼ climb, ◀▶ traverse; ▼ at the sand steps down.

Corner

At a sand-to-glass edge the leading arms climb first (flex bias per arm) and the body pitches 90° over 1.2 s. No physics change: the position is held on a plane.
