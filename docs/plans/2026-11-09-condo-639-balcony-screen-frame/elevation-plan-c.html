How it works

  • Each panel seats against a stop along the outer edge of the top and bottom rails and is latched from inside; it can only leave inward.
  • All six panels are identical, so any one fits any position.
  • Black pet mesh stops pigeons, cats and mosquitoes. It cuts rain spray but does not stop wind-driven rain.
  • v2: six weather inserts (tinted polycarbonate or glass) swap into the same positions while away, gasketed, with rebated joints and a drained sill, so no rain gets in.
  • Solar (A): a custom glass-glass laminate in the insert frame, ≈ 400–450 W for six. (B): a stock 50 W panel low in each insert, clear polycarbonate above, 300 W. The grey band is the ledge's shadow at a 45° sun.
