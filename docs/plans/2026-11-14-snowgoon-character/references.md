# Snowgoon visual reference archive

Research notes for the [Snowgoon character, rig and snowball actions plan](../2026-11-14-snowgoon-character.md).
The visual source is Bill Watterson's *Calvin & Hobbes* Snow Goons story arc.
This index preserves the publication details, stable links and intended design
use; the copyrighted comic artwork remains at its publisher/archive source.

### Collected edition

The [publisher's listing](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/Attack-of-the-Deranged-Mutant-Killer-Monster-Snow-Goons/Bill-Watterson/Calvin-and-Hobbes/9780836218831)
is for the 1992 collection, ISBN 9780836218831. [A contents index](https://www.sentex.ca/~ajy/snowgoons.html)
lists the Snow Goons story on pages 85–90 and related snowmen material on
pages 91–92 and 101. The collection spans a much wider date range than the
January 1991 goon story, so the archive below is scoped to strips in that story
sequence, not every snowman strip in the book.

The cover is saved for local personal research and embedded in the design plan:
`attack-of-the-deranged-mutant-killer-monster-snow-goons-cover.jpg`.

## Reference notes

- GoComics is the official strip-reading source linked here. Some strip images
  may require an account/subscription depending on access and region.
- The January 18 scan is a complete newspaper page, not a standalone strip
  image. Its search-indexed text identifies the page as Friday, January 18,
  1991; the PDF is retained at the archive link rather than copied into this
  repository.
- The January 1991 sequence is part of the Snow Goons arc collected in the
  publisher-listed book. See the [arc index](https://www.sentex.ca/~ajy/snowgoons.html)
  for a date/topic finding aid.
- Treat the comics as visual reference. The game character should keep its
  existing twin-headed snowman identity while receiving original facial
  details, proportions, rig design and animations.
