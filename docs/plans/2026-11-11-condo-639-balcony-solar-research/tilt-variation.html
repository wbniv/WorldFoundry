The soffit is the underside of the ledge, which runs 35 cm inward from the flush outside wall (Will, 2026‑11‑11). A vertical panel at the outer edge of the cap has nothing above it outside, so it is never shaded. Tilting the top inward faces the panel a little more toward the sky, but moves its top under the soffit, which shades it whenever the sun is high. In option B the top 9 cm of each insert is clear polycarbonate, so a shadow shallower than that costs nothing.
