Three additive changes, all in wflevels/condo_639_640/blender_create_condo.py: (1) a second Directional fill light in the engine's idle 2nd slot (of 3, see wfsource/source/gfx/glpipeline/backend_modern.cc RB_MAX_LIGHTS) so walls facing away from the sun still get a directional gradient instead of flat ambient; (2) generalising the existing darken_floors() per‑face tint (already shipping for floors) to also darken wall/ceiling and wall/wall seam strips, faking the contact‑AO this vertex‑lit engine can't compute; (3) a small ambient trim (0.45→~0.38) so the two directional lights read with more contrast. No shadow‑mapping or engine changes required.
