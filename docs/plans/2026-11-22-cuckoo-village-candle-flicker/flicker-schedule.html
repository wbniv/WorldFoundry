The flicker rule, run live

Each of the 24 groups (12 trees x 2) flips between shape 1 and shape 2 when sin(2π(2.3 t + p)) + sin(2π(3.7 t + 2p)) > 0, with its own phase p. Gold = shape 1, blue = shape 2. The ruler is the 30 fps frame the Chromecast shows.
