Cuckoo Village — Christmas candles, 1 December to 6 January

Schematic mockup of the trees only, not a rendering. Candles are a date-driven visibility switch; the glow follows the day, dusk and night blend and the Window lights setting.

Out of season

Before 1 December, or after 6 January: bare trees, and the candle meshes are hidden

In season, by day

Candles stand unlit: small cream sticks, no glow, no light cast

In season, dusk and night

Flames fade on with the window blend, each at its own fixed offset, with a tiny deterministic flicker
