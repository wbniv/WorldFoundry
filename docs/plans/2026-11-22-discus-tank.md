# Discus tank

The twelfth Aquarium entry adds 30 brown discus (*Symphysodon aequifasciatus*): a player and 29 residents using the existing schooling controller. The tank adapts the freshwater shell and sand to a 29-inch height and 27-inch waterline, and has a screensaver variant with an automatic player. Existing menu indices remain unchanged.

The fish geometry and photographic texture are in `wflevels/discus_asset`; see its README for source attribution and licensing. The tank generator and movement live in `wflevels/aquarium`, and `wflevels/aquarium_discus/build.sh` rebuilds the level. The default level settings provide the framerate toggle. The design sketch is [here](2026-11-22-discus-tank/discus-tank.png).

Verification must cover the cooked level in the engine, directional input and release, schooling, the chooser and screensaver, and performance on a Chromecast. `tests/test_aquarium_discus.py` checks the roster, bounds, assets, scripts and screensaver wiring; desktop captures can be made with `docs/diagnostics/aquarium-discus-2026-11-22/capture.py`. The 2026-11-23 refinement uses approximately 7.5 cm fish, slower swimming and turns, a reduced tank-only mesh and a 20 MB room budget. The PC standalone, chooser and screensaver-bundle checks passed; cast1 measured approximately 30 FPS in the steady tank segment. Launcher-tile verification and the Android Dream lifecycle remain open. Cast2 and the Lenovo tablet were unreachable. Evidence: `docs/diagnostics/discus-30fish-lean-profile-2026-11-23/README.md`.
