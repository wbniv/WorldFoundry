Mockup 4: the states, including the failures a living room produces

Phone screens and the TV panel, each with what the user sees and what the code does.
Connecting…

1 Connecting

The page loaded; the WebSocket is opening. Buttons are shown but disabled.

● Connected to the TV

2 Connected

Buttons live. The mask is sent on every change and every 50 ms (the TV's Wi-Fi dozes between packets otherwise).

Reconnecting… buttons off

3 Signal lost

No reply for 1 s: buttons grey out and the phone retries every second. The TV releases every button at once (no stuck RIGHT).

Wrong code. Scan the TV again.

4 Wrong PIN

The PIN in the URL does not match this launch's: HTTP 403 and a plain message. No game input accepted.

Can’t reach the TV.
Same Wi-Fi? Guest network?

5 Not on the same Wi-Fi

Nothing answers within 5 s. The page says why: join the TV's network; a guest network or AP isolation blocks it.

Screen kept awake

6 Phone locks or sleeps

The page keeps the screen awake while connected (a silent looping video: the Wake Lock API needs https). If the phone sleeps anyway, the page releases everything and it is state 3 on the TV.

Another phone took over

7 A second phone joins

The newest connection wins; the first sees “Another phone took over”. Two players is a later feature.

Enable tilt (one-time certificate prompt)

8 Tilt needs a secure page

Browsers expose tilt only to https pages. Tap “Enable tilt”: the page reloads over https from the TV and your browser asks once about the certificate. Sticks and buttons never need this.

Haptics: not on this phone

9 No haptics on iPhone

Safari has no vibration API. The toggle is hidden there and the page says so; Android Chrome buzzes. Game-driven buzzes (door, teleport) arrive from the TV as an h: message.

Remote + phone + gamepad

10 The remote at the same time

Remote, a gamepad and the phone are OR-ed together, like the touch HUD and a gamepad already are. Any of them works.
