# Spike S3: the seed strip and the 85% framing

Plan: [2026‑10‑21 Planted Tank screensaver](../../2026-10-21-planted-tank-screensaver.md), Phase 0d. Read-only investigation on `2026-new-level`, 2026‑10‑21. Nothing was compiled, edited or committed.

**Implementation update (2026‑10‑22):** The later Dream host clears the engine drawer every frame, which hides the strip without changing engine code. The investigation below records what was known before that host path was traced. Desktop and offline checks pass; the device capture remains pending.

## Verdicts

| Question | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Can the screensaver variant drop the `Seed: … \| Hold A: settings` strip **without an engine edit**? | **NO.** The strip is drawn by engine C++ (`planted::buildUI`) on every frame while `planted::state().active` is true. `active` is set by `planted::enter()`, which is what generates the plants, and growth (`planted::tick`) returns early unless `active` is set. No level field, catalog field, object flag, mailbox or host switch (`WFAndroidSetHudEnabled(0)`, `--no-fps`) reaches the strip. |
| Does the plan's `label` parameter (strip only when asked) have a no-engine-edit implementation? | **NO**, for the same reason. It needs a seam of about three lines (below), so it needs Will's say-so. |
| Framing: which camera numbers give at least 85% width? | Move the **wide** camera from `y = -12` to **`y = -8.25`** and keep `x = 0`, `z = 2.5` and the `LookAt` at `(0, 0, 2.5)`. The tank then fills **90.0% of the width and 71.3% of the height** at 16:9, with nothing cropped (a 5% margin each side for TV overscan). The hard limit for 85% is `y = -8.638`. The vertical field of view is fixed at 60° in the engine, so FOV is not a lever. |
| Edit method | A text substitution in `scripts/build-aquarium-screensaver-levels.py`, scoped to two `OBJ` blocks in the exported `.lev`: the `Position` y of `"cs_wide"` and of `"Camera"`. The builder already splits on `\t{ 'OBJ'` to rewrite the Player, so this is the same technique. No engine edit and no change to the canonical level. |
| Does the screensaver stay on the wide camera? | **Yes.** The only switch to the close camera is a native tap of A (`game.cc:677` toggles mailbox 625). The Dream is non-interactive, and the screensaver host forwards no keys to the engine, so mailbox 625 stays 0. |

ESCALATE: the plan's "no strip" goal (Phase 1) and the `label` parameter both need a small engine edit, which needs Will's explicit permission. This is beyond the T3 remit. The rest of Phase 1, including the framing, is independent of it.

### The smallest engine seam for the strip

If Will approves, three edits in two files do it. They are not made here.

1. `wfsource/source/game/plant_settings.h:12`: add `bool label=true;` to `planted::State`.
2. `plant_settings.h:36`, inside the `if(!selected)` block of `enter()` (lines 31-38): `s.label=value(9,"1")!="0";` (field 9 is the next free id after the plan's field 8 for seahorses). A missing field keeps today's behaviour, so the canonical level and the eight Aquarium variants are unchanged.
3. `wfsource/source/game/plant_ui.cc:12`: `if(s.active){` becomes `if(s.active&&s.label){`.

The variant then sets field 9 to `0` in its `PlantedTankSettings` catalog entry. Alternatively, the Phase 2b staging adapter sets `state().label` directly. With that seam, `label` becomes a runtime parameter instead of a cooked one. Note that `enter()` reads catalog fields only when no seed was staged (`if(!selected)`, line 31), so a staged run would also have to stage `label`. This matters for Spike S1.

**What the close camera shows.** A 1:1 chase view about 4.35 m in front of the Player, centred on it. That is the sea urchin in saltwater, or the goby in freshwater: the Player script runs `tk-urchin-player-tick` or `gb-player-tick` on `pg-salt` (`generate.py:498`). It shows a small part of the substrate and the lower plants.

**How A switches it.** In the cooked level the Forth A-tap edge (`plants_controller.fth:14`) is replaced with a comment, because `PLANTED_TANK_DETAIL=runtime` is the default (`generate.py:491-492`). The `.lev` has 0 hits for `JOYSTICK_BUTTON_A tk-edge` and 2 for the replacement comment. The native settings gesture handles A instead: a tap shorter than 1 s toggles mailbox 625 (`game.cc:677`, `engine/runtime_property_host.cpp:133-134`), and a hold of 1 s or more opens the settings modal.

### The calculation

A pinhole model with 60° vertical, 16:9, the camera at `(0, cy, cz)` looking at `(0, 0, cz)`, projecting the 8 corners of the box x ±6.096, y ±1.651, z 0 to 5.434. The widest points are the front corners.

The model reproduces the capture: at `cy = -12` it gives **57.4% width and 45.5% height**. The PNG measures 1102 px of 1920 (57.4%) and 490 px of 1080 (45.4%).

| `cy` (z 2.5) | Width | Vertical NDC (bottom to top) | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| -12 (today) | 57.4% | -0.418 to 0.491 | matches the capture |
| -9.0 | 80.8% | -0.589 to 0.692 | short of target |
| -8.638 | 85.0% | -0.620 to 0.727 | exact 85% limit |
| -8.5 | 86.7% | -0.632 to 0.742 | |
| **-8.25** | **90.0%** | **-0.656 to 0.770** | **recommended**: 96 px margin each side at 1920, 71% of the height |
| -8.107 | 92.0% | -0.671 to 0.787 | tighter. Overscan on some TVs could clip the walls |

Centring vertically (moving both the camera and the `LookAt` to z 2.717, the mid-height of the shell) makes the vertical span symmetric (±0.713 at -8.25) but changes a third object. It is optional and not needed for the target.

**What leaves the frame at `cy = -8.25`.** Only the lower part of the stand: the frame bottom reaches z ≈ -1.2 at the stand's front face, so the stand's bottom 1.3 m is cropped. The black band under the stand in the current capture (below the backdrop's z -5 edge) also leaves the frame: the bottom edge now meets the backdrop at z ≈ -4.0. Nothing in the tank is cropped.

### The variant builder edit (for Phase 1, not made here)

In the exported `.lev`, inside the `OBJ` blocks named `"cs_wide"` and `"Camera"` only, replace `-12.0000000000000000(1.15.16)` in the `Position` VEC3 with `-8.2500000000000000(1.15.16)`. That string occurs exactly twice in the file, at lines 130 and 655, so a check for "exactly one hit per block, two in total" makes the substitution fail loudly if the generator moves the camera. Change `Camera` too, so the level does not open on a one-second pan (`Pan Time In Seconds 1.0`, `.lev:757`) from the old position. Do the substitution in the same pass as `player_script()`, and only for the new `planted_tank_screensaver` target, so that the Aquarium bundle's `aquarium_plants_screensaver` keeps today's framing unless Will wants both.

## Risks

1. **Engine permission.** "No strip" is in Phase 1's scope, and the `label` parameter is in the plan's table, but neither is reachable without the three-line seam. If Will declines, the screensaver ships with the strip, as the Aquarium Dream's Planted Tank does today, and `label` should come out of the parameter table.
2. **Aspect assumption.** The percentages assume a 16:9 surface. On a narrower surface (a 4:3 preview window) the walls can be cropped at `cy = -8.25`: at 4:3 the width would be 120%. The Chromecast HD and any TV Dream are 16:9.
3. **Overscan.** 90% leaves 5% each side. TVs that overscan by more than about 5% would clip the tank walls. Phase 5 should check on the real TV.
4. **Generator drift.** The substitution relies on `-12.0000000000000000(1.15.16)` appearing exactly once in each of the two blocks. The builder must assert this rather than replace blindly.
5. **Untested on device.** All framing numbers come from a model that was validated against one retained capture (57.4% / 45.5% predicted; 57.4% / 45.4% measured). Phase 1 should take a capture to confirm.
